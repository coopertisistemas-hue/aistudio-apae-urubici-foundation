# Partners Module

## Home placement

Partners should appear as a premium section close to the end of the Home, before the final footer area.

## Purpose

Provide institutional recognition without turning APAE into an advertising surface.

## Suggested fields

- name
- logo
- category
- short_description
- website
- instagram
- portal_profile_url
- supported_projects
- featured
- display_order
- partnership_start
- partnership_end
- status

## Presentation & Click Destination Contract

Prefer a calm and elegant logo presentation with optional featured-partner storytelling and a link to a full Partners page.

### Primary Link Contract
For a published partner, the primary click destination from the APAE public site is strictly the corresponding `portal_profile_url` on Portal Urubici.
- `website` and `instagram` may remain non-primary metadata in directory details, but are not the primary outbound destination from public cards/logos.
- If `portal_profile_url` is missing or unvalidated, the public partner card and logo must fail closed or remain non-clickable to prevent directing traffic away from the governed civic ecosystem.
