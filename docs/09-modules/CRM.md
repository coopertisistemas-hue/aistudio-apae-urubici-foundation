# CRM / Relationship Module

## Purpose
Turn legitimate site interactions into organized institutional relationships without covert tracking or forced marketing consent.

## Relationship types
- donor
- recurring supporter
- volunteer
- potential partner
- partner contact
- newsletter subscriber
- testimonial contributor
- general contact

## Suggested data
- name
- email
- WhatsApp/phone
- source
- interests
- relationship type
- consent state
- last interaction
- internal follow-up status

## Principles
- consent-aware;
- minimal data;
- clear source attribution;
- human follow-up remains possible;
- avoid intrusive profiling.

## Future
Segmentation, campaign sends and engagement analytics can be added after governance and provider choices are approved.
