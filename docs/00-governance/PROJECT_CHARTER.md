# Project Charter

## Product

APAE Urubici Digital Platform

## Purpose

Build a premium, mobile-first, accessible, content-rich and administrable digital presence for APAE Urubici, including a public website and a professional Admin/CMS.

The platform must support institutional communication, impact storytelling, projects, campaigns, testimonials, partners, transparency, donations, relationship capture, editorial content, and future integrations.

## Product authority

This Foundation repository is canonical.

If implementation output conflicts with this Foundation, the Foundation wins unless an explicit decision changes the Foundation first.

## Product surfaces

1. Public Site
2. Admin/CMS
3. Supabase backend and storage
4. Edge Functions and integrations
5. Portal Urubici integration
6. Donations and relationship flows
7. Social and communication integrations

## Core principles

- Mobile-first is P0.
- Desktop must also be intentionally composed and premium.
- Accessibility is P0.
- No fabricated institutional claims, statistics, testimonials, partners, or impact data.
- Dynamic content must be Admin-driven where specified.
- Sensitive publication requires governance and consent.
- Premium quality applies to both Site and Admin.
- Implementation agents execute; they do not redefine the product silently.
