# APAE Urubici — Brand Identity Direction

Status: W01P PROPOSAL STANDARD — final institutional approval remains required before production certification.

## Source identity

The current APAE Urubici logo supplied for the proposal is the visual source of truth for the digital revitalization direction.

Core elements to preserve:
- sunflower / yellow flower;
- protective hands;
- green stem and leaves;
- APAE Urubici institutional identification;
- symbolism of care, dignity, growth, inclusion and human development.

The objective is not to invent a new institution or discard the existing identity. The objective is to create a cleaner, contemporary, digital-ready interpretation suitable for web, mobile, favicon, social preview and future administrative surfaces.

## Required digital variants

The proposal brand system should prepare:
- primary logo;
- compact/reduced logo;
- horizontal lockup where useful;
- monochrome/light-dark variants where accessibility requires;
- favicon/app icon derived from the most recognizable brand symbol;
- vector-ready master asset for future institutional approval.

## Visual character

The digital identity must feel:
- human;
- luminous;
- optimistic;
- welcoming;
- dignified;
- contemporary;
- trustworthy;
- accessible.

Avoid:
- childish visual language;
- generic NGO aesthetics;
- charity/pity framing;
- arbitrary colors unrelated to the APAE Urubici logo;
- excessive decorative effects.

## Proposal rule

Any revitalized logo is a proposal asset until APAE Urubici formally approves it.

The implementation may use a proposal-grade digital treatment, but must preserve easy replacement if the institution requests adjustments.
