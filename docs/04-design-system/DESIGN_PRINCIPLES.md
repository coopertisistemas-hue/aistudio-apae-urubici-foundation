# Design Principles

## Direction

Human, luminous, contemporary, welcoming and institutional.

## Brand alignment

The public Site must visibly belong to APAE Urubici.

The supplied APAE Urubici logo is the visual anchor. The interface should harmonize its sunflower yellow, leaf green and warm neutral/gray characteristics into a premium accessible system rather than introducing an unrelated color identity.

Logo revitalization must preserve the recognizable institutional symbolism while improving digital clarity and versatility.

## Rules

- Mobile-first composition.
- Desktop intentionally designed, never a stretched mobile layout.
- APAE Urubici identity respected and visibly reflected in the interface.
- Neutral surfaces should feel sophisticated and calm.
- Photography and video should feel authentic.
- Decorative effects must never reduce accessibility or readability.
- Motion must be restrained and purposeful.
- Content hierarchy must be clear within the first viewport.
- Language selection must work coherently in both desktop and mobile navigation.

## Premium quality

Premium does not mean visual excess. It means excellent hierarchy, spacing, typography, media treatment, interaction quality and consistency.
