# Design Principles

## Direction

Human, luminous, contemporary, welcoming and institutional.

## Rules

- Mobile-first composition.
- Desktop intentionally designed, never a stretched mobile layout.
- APAE identity respected without overusing institutional blue.
- Neutral surfaces should feel sophisticated and calm.
- Photography and video should feel authentic.
- Decorative effects must never reduce accessibility or readability.
- Motion must be restrained and purposeful.
- Content hierarchy must be clear within the first viewport.

## Premium quality

Premium does not mean visual excess. It means excellent hierarchy, spacing, typography, media treatment, interaction quality and consistency.
