# Color System

Status: W01P PROPOSAL STANDARD — final W02 token freeze and institutional approval remain required.

## Brand-derived direction

The APAE Urubici digital palette must be harmonized from the supplied institutional logo rather than from an unrelated visual theme.

Reference colors extracted/derived from the supplied logo:

- Sunflower Yellow: approximately `#FFDD00`
- Warm Golden Yellow: approximately `#FFB813`
- Leaf Green: approximately `#5CB95E`
- Warm Hand Gray: approximately `#BFBBAF`

Supporting digital colors may be derived to provide hierarchy and accessibility, for example:

- Deep Green: approximately `#27683A`
- Warm Ivory / Off-white: approximately `#FAF7EE`
- Charcoal: approximately `#20251F`

These values are proposal references, not yet a frozen institutional brand manual.

## Intent

Yellow should communicate energy, hope and welcome.

Green should communicate growth, development, inclusion and community.

Gray/neutrals should provide maturity and balance.

The interface must feel premium and harmonious, not saturated.

## Rules

- Use the APAE Urubici logo as the color anchor.
- Do not retain arbitrary terracotta/moss choices if they conflict with the logo-derived system.
- Do not flood large surfaces with bright yellow.
- Use darker greens/charcoal for accessible text and contrast.
- Maintain WCAG-oriented contrast for text, controls and focus states.
- Functional states must remain consistent across Site and future Admin.
- Final production tokens are frozen only after W02/institutional review.
