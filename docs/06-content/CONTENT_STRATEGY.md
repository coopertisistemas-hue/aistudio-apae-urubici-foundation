# Content Strategy

## Purpose
Keep the site alive through useful, credible and mission-aligned content.

## Content pillars
1. Institutional trust
2. What APAE does
3. Projects and impact
4. Inclusion & Human Development
5. Community and events
6. Ways to help
7. Partners and collaboration
8. Transparency
9. Stories/testimonials with consent

## Cadence
The platform should support continuous publication without requiring developer intervention.

## Editorial distinction
APAE institutional content and Portal Urubici editorial content must remain clearly attributed.

## Avoid
- empty SEO filler;
- copied institutional text without validation;
- sensational beneficiary stories;
- content published solely to satisfy frequency.
