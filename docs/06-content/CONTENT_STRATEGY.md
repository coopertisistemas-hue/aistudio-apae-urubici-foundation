# Content Strategy

## Purpose
Keep the site alive through useful, credible and mission-aligned content.

## Content pillars
1. Institutional trust
2. What APAE does
3. Projects and impact
4. Inclusion & Human Development
5. Community and events
6. Ways to help
7. Partners and collaboration
8. Transparency
9. Stories/testimonials with consent

## Cadence
The platform should support continuous publication without requiring developer intervention.

## Editorial distinction
APAE institutional content and Portal Urubici editorial content must remain clearly attributed.

## Avoid
- empty SEO filler;
- copied institutional text without validation;
- sensational beneficiary stories;
- content published solely to satisfy frequency.


## Rights-based institutional communication

Content should explain not only care and support, but also:
- rights;
- autonomy;
- citizenship;
- accessibility;
- participation;
- belonging;
- human development.

APAE Urubici should not be framed only as charity, school or clinic.

## Social content as evidence and distribution

Instagram, Facebook and other public channels should be inventoried to understand current activities, services, events, campaigns and community relationships.

Rules:
- social posts are not automatically canonical institutional facts;
- recent claims should be cross-checked;
- publication rights/consent must be reviewed before media reuse;
- the Site remains the authoritative institutional hub;
- social platforms should feed discovery and community connection rather than define the content model.

## Accessibility of content

Editorial content should support cognitive accessibility through:
- concise summaries;
- descriptive headings;
- plain/direct language where possible;
- structured lists and FAQs for complex information;
- alternative text and media descriptions;
- captions/transcripts for audiovisual content.


## Protagonism and family framing

Content must prefer:
- voice over representation-by-others;
- agency over passivity;
- ability/talent/choice over deficit framing;
- belonging over charity;
- practical orientation for families over institutional jargon;
- rights and participation over pity.

Future participant stories must be consent-governed and center the person's own voice whenever possible.

Family is a primary audience and a cross-cutting content axis.

"Acontece na APAE" should become the future living editorial layer for validated news, events, campaigns, culture, sport, activities and community moments.
