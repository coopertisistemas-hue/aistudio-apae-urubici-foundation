# Voice and Tone

## Voice
Human, respectful, clear, hopeful, credible and professional.

## Tone
- dignified, never paternalistic;
- warm, never sentimentalized;
- accessible, never simplistic;
- confident, never exaggerated;
- transparent about facts and limitations.

## Disability language
Use respectful, current and institutionally approved language. Avoid framing people primarily through limitation, pity or inspiration narratives.

## Calls to action
Prefer clear action language:
- Conheça nosso trabalho
- Faça parte
- Apoie este projeto
- Seja parceiro
- Compartilhe sua experiência

## Data claims
Only publish validated numbers and traceable institutional claims.
