# Editorial Model

## APAE-origin content
- news;
- projects;
- campaigns;
- events;
- institutional updates;
- stories.

## Portal-origin content
Selected Inclusion & Human Development articles may be surfaced through governed integration.

## Institutional editorial — Palavra da Presidência
- "Palavra da Presidência" refers to APAE institutional editorial content authored by the APAE president.
- It is distinct from the Portal Urubici editorial axis and from Aline Liz.
- Specific author identity, photography, and text must be institutionally validated before publication.

## Workflow
Draft → review → publish → optional distribution.

## Metadata
Content should support:
- title;
- slug;
- excerpt;
- body;
- cover media;
- author/source;
- category/topics;
- publication date;
- SEO metadata;
- status;
- related content;
- distribution eligibility.

## Moderation
User-submitted content remains pending until reviewed.
