# Retention and Privacy

## Principles
- data minimization;
- purpose limitation;
- least privilege;
- clear retention rationale;
- auditable consent;
- deletion/anonymization workflows where legally and operationally appropriate.

## Public beneficiaries
Avoid collecting or exposing unnecessary health, disability or family-sensitive data through public marketing features.

## CRM
Store only relationship information needed for legitimate follow-up.

## Payments
Do not store raw card data. Rely on payment providers for regulated payment details.

## Logs
Avoid placing sensitive personal data or secrets in application logs.

## Policy
A final LGPD/privacy policy and retention schedule must be reviewed before production certification.
