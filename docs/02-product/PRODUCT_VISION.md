# Product Vision

## Vision

A digital platform worthy of APAE Urubici's institutional work: human, credible, accessible, transparent, alive and easy to operate.

## Desired perception

The experience should communicate:
- dignity;
- inclusion;
- trust;
- warmth;
- professionalism;
- transparency;
- hope;
- community.

It must avoid:
- infantilized visual language;
- outdated charity-site aesthetics;
- excessive corporate coldness;
- inaccessible motion or interactions;
- generic CRUD-like Admin experiences.

## Strategic value

The platform should serve APAE Urubici first while also becoming a high-quality social-impact case for Urubici Connect.
