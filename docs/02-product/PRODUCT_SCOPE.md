# Product Scope

## In scope

### Public Site
Premium institutional and editorial website with:
- Home;
- institutional history and purpose;
- areas of work;
- projects and campaigns;
- news and articles;
- events;
- testimonials;
- partners;
- transparency;
- contact;
- donation and participation journeys;
- Portal Urubici editorial integration.

### Admin/CMS
Professional operational workspace for:
- homepage and hero;
- institutional data;
- projects/campaigns;
- articles/news/events;
- testimonials and moderation;
- partners;
- transparency documents;
- contacts/relationship capture;
- donors/donations/recurrences;
- media;
- distribution/integration settings.

### Platform
- Supabase database;
- Auth;
- RLS;
- managed storage;
- Edge Functions;
- payment integrations;
- email automation;
- future WhatsApp and Meta integrations.

## Progressive scope

Can be implemented after first production release without redesigning the architecture:
- advanced CRM segmentation;
- newsletter campaigns;
- automated social publishing;
- richer analytics;
- donor impact reports;
- partner portal;
- workflow approvals beyond basic moderation.

## Out of scope unless explicitly approved

- open-ended page builder;
- automatic publication of unmoderated user content;
- invented impact metrics;
- uncontrolled AI-generated institutional claims;
- exposing sensitive beneficiary information;
- public access to financial operational details beyond approved transparency content.
