# Audiences and Operational Personas

## Public audiences

### Families and community
Need trustworthy information, services, events, rights, contact and useful inclusion content.

### Donors and supporters
Need confidence, clear donation journeys, transparency and evidence of institutional impact.

### Potential partners
Need credibility, partnership options, recognition and evidence of responsible governance.

### Volunteers
Need a simple path to express interest and understand opportunities.

### Media and community leaders
Need reliable institutional information, current news and official contacts.

### People seeking inclusion and human-development content
Need accessible, educational and respectful editorial content.

## Operational personas

### APAE content operator
Creates and updates content without technical knowledge.

### APAE manager/president
Needs oversight, publication confidence, donation visibility, partner information and high-level indicators.

### Finance/authorized fundraising user
Needs donation/payment visibility without unnecessary content-management privileges.

### Urubici Connect administrator
Maintains platform health, integrations, configuration and technical governance.

## Design implication

Public UX must optimize simplicity and trust. Admin UX must optimize safety, clarity, autonomy and low training cost.
