# Functional Requirements

## Public Site
- Responsive navigation and accessible menu.
- Admin-driven homepage sections.
- Dedicated pages for major Home subjects.
- Video hero with mobile/desktop variants and fallbacks.
- Dynamic projects, campaigns, news, articles and events.
- Moderated testimonials.
- Partner showcase near footer.
- Donation journeys.
- Contact and relationship forms.
- Transparency document publishing.
- Portal Urubici editorial consumption with graceful fallback.
- SEO/AEO metadata and structured content.

## Admin
- Authentication and role-aware navigation.
- Dashboard.
- CRUD with draft/published/archived lifecycle where applicable.
- Homepage configuration without free-form layout destruction.
- Media upload and metadata.
- Testimonial moderation and consent evidence.
- Donation/provider visibility.
- Partner and project association.
- Transparency document management.
- Search/filter/sort for operational lists.
- Preview before publication for high-impact surfaces.
- Audit-relevant timestamps and actor data.

## Communications
- Professional automatic thank-you email after confirmed donation.
- Confirmation/receipt-like communications where legally/operationally appropriate.
- Invitation link for testimonials.
- Future newsletter, WhatsApp and social distribution compatibility.

## Data integrity
- No public unpublished content.
- No automatic testimonial publication.
- No client exposure of service-role or other privileged secrets.
- Consent records immutable enough for audit needs.
