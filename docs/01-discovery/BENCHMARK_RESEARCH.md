# Benchmark Research — W00

## Objective

Identify useful patterns from APAE organizations and mature social-impact organizations without copying their design or content.

## Patterns worth carrying forward

### Donations
- One-time and recurring contributions.
- Suggested values plus custom amount.
- PIX and card/payment-provider support.
- Thank-you communication after confirmed payment.
- Donation history visible in Admin.

### Relationship
- Distinct consent for marketing/relationship.
- Email and WhatsApp capture.
- Volunteer and partner interest forms.
- Donor relationship lifecycle.

### Testimonials
- Public form and invitation-link flows.
- Moderation before publication.
- Explicit consent for name, text, photo and social reuse.

### Partners
- Dedicated partner model.
- Partner recognition close to the footer on the Home.
- Optional featured partner and project association.

### Transparency
- Statutes.
- annual reports;
- financial statements;
- agreements;
- institutional documents.

### Content
- News.
- projects;
- campaigns;
- events;
- educational articles;
- inclusion and human development content.

### Accessibility
- Mobile-first interaction.
- readable typography.
- sufficient touch targets.
- reduced motion support.
- keyboard and assistive-technology consideration.

## Product differentiation

The opportunity for APAE Urubici is not one isolated feature. It is the integration of these capabilities into one premium, administrable and mobile-first experience.
