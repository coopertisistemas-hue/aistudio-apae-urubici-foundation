# APAE Brasil — Reference Research for APAE Urubici

Status: PRODUCT / EXPERIENCE REFERENCE — NOT A LAYOUT COPYING SPECIFICATION

Date: 2026-09-27

Reference:
https://apaebrasil.org.br/

## Purpose

Use APAE Brasil as a conceptual and institutional reference for the new APAE Urubici public experience.

This reference does NOT authorize copying:
- page layout;
- component composition;
- proprietary visual assets;
- campaign artwork;
- copy;
- illustrations;
- navigation structure.

The goal is to learn from the living character of the national movement and translate that into a distinctive, premium, accessible local experience.

## Observed reference characteristics

### 1. A living institution

APAE Brasil prominently surfaces:
- campaigns;
- news;
- donation;
- events and movement activity;
- family participation;
- inclusion and rights;
- self-advocacy and representation.

Product implication:
APAE Urubici should feel alive and active, not like a static institutional brochure.

### 2. Protagonism instead of pity

Current APAE Brasil material for the 2026 National Week uses the theme:
"Deficiência não define. Oportunidade transforma. Inclua nossa voz!"

It explicitly emphasizes:
- listening to people with disabilities;
- respecting wishes and choices;
- recognizing spoken and non-spoken forms of expression;
- removing social, attitudinal, communication and structural barriers;
- "Nada sobre nós, sem nós".

Product implication:
The Urubici experience must foreground voice, agency, talent, choice, belonging and participation.

Never use disability as spectacle or sentimental conversion device.

### 3. Family as a structural axis

APAE Brasil's 2026 "Ano da Família Apaeana" positions family as a cross-cutting institutional axis around:
- presence;
- participation;
- support;
- belonging.

Product implication:
Parents, guardians and families are primary users of the public Site, not an afterthought.

The experience should answer practical questions quickly and create emotional safety without paternalism.

### 4. Campaign and editorial energy

APAE Brasil maintains active campaign and news surfaces.

Product implication:
Create a future-ready "Acontece na APAE" model combining:
- news;
- events;
- campaigns;
- culture;
- sport;
- family/community moments;
- achievements;
- institutional updates.

For proposal mode, never invent real current items.

### 5. Rights and accessible understanding

APAE Brasil's content ecosystem includes rights, self-advocacy and inclusion education.

Product implication:
APAE Urubici should evolve toward a useful rights/information product, with:
- plain language;
- strong visual hierarchy;
- short explanations;
- accessible definitions;
- future audio/Libras support where validated and feasible.

## New creative direction

The APAE Urubici experience must be:

- joyful without trivializing;
- playful without infantilizing;
- colorful without visual overload;
- premium without becoming cold;
- emotionally warm without pity;
- editorial without becoming bureaucratic;
- accessible without creating a separate "simplified" second-class experience;
- highly shareable without engagement gimmicks.

## Visual language

Derive the visual grammar from the APAE Urubici brand itself:

- flower;
- petals;
- leaves;
- growth;
- organic curves;
- warm circles;
- soft asymmetric framing;
- playful but restrained motion.

Use these as a design system, not decorative noise.

The brand colors should feel alive across the experience rather than being limited to CTA buttons.

## Age and dignity

The Site may be visually inviting to children and families, but APAE is not only for children.

The visual system must preserve dignity and relevance for:
- children;
- adolescents;
- adults;
- older persons;
- families;
- professionals;
- community.

Do not use childish typography, nursery-school tropes, cartoon stereotypes or infantilizing copy for adults with disabilities.

## Signature experience ideas

### "Como podemos ajudar você hoje?"

A prominent user-intent gateway may offer pathways such as:
- conhecer os serviços;
- buscar orientação;
- entender direitos;
- falar com a APAE;
- participar;
- ajudar.

Final labels and operational routes remain subject to institutional validation.

### "Acontece na APAE"

Future living-content surface for validated:
- activities;
- events;
- stories;
- culture;
- sport;
- campaigns;
- news;
- community participation.

### Stories with agency

Future stories should center:
- voice;
- decisions;
- talents;
- achievements;
- relationships;
- participation.

Never frame beneficiaries as passive recipients of charity.

## Accessibility as experience language

Accessibility is not a compliance badge or overlay.

The visual/product language should support:
- cognitive clarity;
- low digital literacy;
- keyboard navigation;
- screen readers;
- zoom/reflow;
- high-clarity focus;
- reduced motion;
- large meaningful touch targets;
- captions/transcripts;
- future strategic Libras support;
- plain-language content;
- no time-pressure interactions;
- multiple ways to understand essential information.

## Media direction

When institutionally authorized real APAE Urubici media becomes available, prioritize:
- participation;
- friendships;
- family;
- art;
- music;
- movement;
- sport;
- learning;
- daily life;
- achievements;
- community.

Media governance and consent remain mandatory.

Public availability never equals reuse authorization.
