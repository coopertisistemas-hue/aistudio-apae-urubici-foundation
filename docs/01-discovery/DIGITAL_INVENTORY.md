# Digital Inventory — W00

## Scope

Initial public inventory of APAE Urubici's current digital presence and content opportunities.

## Current presence

APAE Urubici has an existing public institutional presence with historical, mission, values, contact, gallery and statutory information. The current experience is legacy-oriented and does not represent the target product quality or functional ambition.

## Public content opportunities identified

Public sources indicate material relevant to:
- institutional history;
- education, health and social assistance;
- cultural and artistic participation;
- community events;
- projects supported by public or social funding;
- transparency and public agreements;
- local impact and achievements.

## Data quality caution

Leadership, current team composition, current service numbers, donation details, partners and institutional statistics must be validated directly with APAE Urubici before publication.

## Product opportunity

The target should not be a redesign of the legacy site. It should be a new digital platform combining:
- premium institutional presence;
- living content;
- donations;
- relationship capture;
- testimonials;
- partners;
- transparency;
- projects and campaigns;
- editorial content;
- Portal Urubici connection;
- social distribution;
- Admin autonomy.

## Publication caution

Images involving users, families, minors or people with disabilities must not be reused automatically from public sources. Publication requires provenance and appropriate consent/authorization.
