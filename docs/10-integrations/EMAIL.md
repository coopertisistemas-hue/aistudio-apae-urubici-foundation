# Email

## Transactional priorities
- donation thank-you;
- relevant donation/payment communication;
- testimonial invitation;
- form acknowledgement where useful;
- operational notifications if approved.

## Quality
Messages must be professional, branded, mobile-readable and accessible.

## Tracking
Admin/backend should be able to retain delivery event status where operationally useful without excessive tracking.

## Marketing
Newsletter/promotional email requires appropriate consent and unsubscribe/suppression behavior.

## Templates
Transactional templates should be versioned and editable through governed configuration when practical.
