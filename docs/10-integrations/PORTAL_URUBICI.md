# Portal Urubici Integration

## Bridges

### 1. Editorial bridge
A natural connection between APAE Urubici and Portal Urubici is the editorial axis **Inclusão & Desenvolvimento Humano**.
- Selected Portal articles may be surfaced on the APAE site with explicit source attribution and links to Portal Urubici.
- APAE content may also be selectively exposed to Portal Urubici when institutionally appropriate.

### 2. Partner ecosystem bridge
APAE published partners link directly to their corresponding institutional/business profiles on Portal Urubici (`portal_profile_url`).
- Reinforces the local civic, cultural, and commercial ecosystem of Urubici.
- Avoids routing visitors to unverified external websites while supporting community business partners.

## Principles

- Do not duplicate editorial ownership unnecessarily.
- Preserve source attribution.
- Integrate through governed API/Edge Function contracts.
- Avoid coupling Site availability to Portal availability without graceful fallback.
