# W01 — Institutional Validation and Content Acquisition Plan

## Objective

Collect and validate the institutional inputs required to safely finalize the APAE Urubici Design System, content architecture, data contracts and Readdy implementation context.

W01 must convert open institutional assumptions into approved evidence.

## Authority

Canonical decision register:
`docs/00-governance/DEFERRED_DECISIONS.md`

W01 may resolve institutional and provider/account decisions owned by APAE / Urubici Connect.

W01 must not invent or infer institutional facts.

## Primary stakeholder

APAE Urubici President / authorized institutional representative.

## Required outputs

1. Validated institutional profile.
2. Approved brand assets and usage references.
3. Current leadership.
4. Current service/areas-of-work inventory.
5. Validated impact indicators.
6. Current partner list and recognition rules.
7. Donation/payment operational details.
8. Approved media inventory + provenance/consent status.
9. Transparency-document inventory.
10. Official digital channels and account ownership.
11. Portal editorial bridge approval.
12. Analytics/privacy decision inputs.
13. Formal W01 readiness report.

## Evidence classes

### A — Authoritative institutional evidence
Provided directly by APAE leadership or official institutional documents.

### B — Official public evidence
Government/Federation/official APAE sources, used only when current and confirmed.

### C — Supporting reference
Public website/social/media material that requires APAE validation before becoming canonical.

## Validation rules

Every canonical institutional value should include:
- value;
- source/evidence;
- approved by;
- validation date;
- publication scope;
- notes/restrictions.

## Stop conditions

Do not mark a deferred decision resolved when:
- information conflicts;
- authorization is unclear;
- media rights are unknown;
- the stakeholder is unsure;
- legal/privacy review is required;
- payment account ownership is not confirmed.

Use `PENDING_VALIDATION` instead.

## W01 completion rule

W01 is complete only when all decisions required for W02 are either:
- RESOLVED, or
- explicitly ACCEPTED_AS_DEFERRED without blocking the Design System and public-site prototype.

Technical decisions owned by later waves may remain OPEN.
