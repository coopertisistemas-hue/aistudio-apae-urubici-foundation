# W01R — APAE Urubici Institutional & Digital Research Baseline

Status: RESEARCH BASELINE — evidence-backed public-source inventory; institutional confirmation still required before production publication.

Date: 2026-09-27

## Purpose

Create a factual, current and usable institutional baseline for the APAE Urubici digital platform before Phase 2 internal-page implementation.

The Site must evolve from a visually strong proposal into a credible digital institutional platform for inclusion and human development in Urubici.

Research must prioritize:
- real current services and areas of work;
- institutional history and mission;
- health, education and social-assistance roles;
- rights, autonomy and community participation;
- art, culture and talents;
- current contacts and social channels;
- projects, campaigns, partners and public actions;
- media provenance and publication rights;
- accessibility for people with disabilities, families and caregivers.

## Evidence policy

Public evidence is classified as:

- **CONFIRMED PUBLIC EVIDENCE** — official public body or current authoritative registry.
- **STRONG SECONDARY EVIDENCE** — current registry/aggregator explicitly sourced from an authoritative database.
- **HISTORICAL EVIDENCE** — useful for history but not automatically representative of current operations.
- **SOCIAL EVIDENCE** — useful to understand activities and communication, but must be cross-checked before being treated as institutional fact.
- **INSTITUTIONAL CONFIRMATION REQUIRED** — must be confirmed directly by APAE Urubici before production publication.

No research finding overrides direct institutional validation when the institution provides newer authoritative information.

## Current recommended institutional framing

The Site should position APAE Urubici around **Inclusion and Human Development across life and community**, rather than presenting it only as a school, clinic or charity.

Proposed communication axes for validation:

1. **Education and Development**
   Learning, development, pedagogical support and individual potential.
   **Evidence update (W01R-02):** official FNDE/FUNDEB records support recent education/AEE activity for Escola Pe. José Gonçalves Espíndola (INEP 42119995) in Urubici. The exact legal/operational relationship with APAE Urubici, current delivery model and access flow remain institutionally unconfirmed.

2. **Health and Rehabilitation**
   Multidisciplinary support, rehabilitation, wellbeing and autonomy, integrated with the public-health network where applicable.

3. **Social Assistance and Families**
   Special social protection, family support, guidance, rights and access to public policy.

4. **Rights, Autonomy and Inclusion**
   Defense and guarantee of rights, citizenship, participation, accessibility and social inclusion.

5. **Art, Culture and Talents**
   Artistic expression, music, culture, sport and opportunities for protagonism.

6. **Community and Participation**
   Families, partners, volunteers, public institutions and community networks.

These axes are a research-backed content architecture proposal. They are not yet final institutional service labels.

## Social-media research requirement

Before content hardening, perform a dedicated Instagram/Facebook and public-content inventory covering preferably the most recent 12–24 months.

For each post/activity, classify:
- date;
- channel;
- activity/service axis;
- event/campaign/project;
- people/roles shown;
- partner/public-body references;
- media reuse suitability;
- rights/consent risk;
- whether it supports a current institutional fact;
- destination Site module/page.

Social content is evidence of communication/activity, not automatically canonical institutional truth.

## Digital-presence objective

The public Site should be the authoritative institutional hub.

Social networks should act as living distribution/community channels, not as the only source of institutional information.

Future Site integration should support:
- latest institutional news/events;
- campaign and community updates;
- links back to verified social posts;
- accessible sharing;
- graceful fallback when social platforms are unavailable;
- no heavy dependency on opaque third-party embeds.

## Content principles

The Site must communicate:
- dignity;
- rights;
- autonomy;
- belonging;
- participation;
- development;
- family and community;
- concrete institutional services;
- transparency and trust.

Avoid:
- pity narratives;
- infantilization;
- vague charity language;
- generic NGO copy;
- unverified claims;
- turning beneficiaries into promotional objects.

## Gate before Phase 2 content hardening

Phase 2 may build page structure only after this research baseline is independently validated.

Production-grade institutional content requires:
1. evidence review;
2. APAE confirmation for unresolved items;
3. media-rights review;
4. accessibility/content review;
5. Foundation update.


## Live proposal deployment verification

Any publicly reachable W01P deployment must be explicitly verified for:
- `noindex, nofollow`;
- visible proposal/non-production labeling;
- no production-only institutional claims;
- no accidental canonical production metadata.

Verification must be repeated after deploy changes that affect HTML metadata or routing.
