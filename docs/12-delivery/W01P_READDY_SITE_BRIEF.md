# W01P — Readdy Public Site Proposal Brief

## Mission

Build a premium, mobile-first proposal website for APAE Urubici that can be shown to institutional leadership as a tangible vision of the future digital platform.

This is a proposal/demo implementation, not a production certification.

## Mandatory experience

The Site must feel:
- human;
- luminous;
- contemporary;
- welcoming;
- dignified;
- trustworthy;
- premium;
- accessible.

Avoid:
- generic NGO templates;
- childish treatment;
- pity/sensationalism;
- cold corporate visuals;
- excessive gradients/effects;
- inaccessible animation;
- dense card walls.

## Homepage

1. Premium hero
2. Institutional introduction
3. Impact structure
4. Areas of work
5. Projects and campaigns
6. Stories/testimonials
7. APAE news
8. Inclusion & Human Development
9. How to help
10. Donation emphasis
11. Partners
12. Final CTA
13. Footer

## Mobile-first

Design mobile first.

Desktop must be independently composed and premium, not a stretched mobile page.

## Proposal data

Do not invent real APAE Urubici facts.

Use safe proposal content that demonstrates:
- hierarchy;
- tone;
- module behavior;
- CTA structure;
- editorial possibilities.

Do not invent:
- current president;
- board;
- service counts;
- beneficiary counts;
- partners;
- donation totals;
- specific impact claims;
- testimonials attributed to real people.

## Internal pages

At minimum propose coherent page experiences for:
- A APAE
- O que fazemos
- Projetos
- Notícias & Artigos
- Eventos
- Como ajudar
- Transparência
- Contato

Additional route shells may follow the Foundation sitemap.

## Hero

Prepare architecture for:
- desktop video;
- mobile video;
- desktop poster;
- mobile poster;
- reduced-motion fallback;
- headline/subheadline;
- primary/secondary CTA;
- overlay controls.

If no rights-cleared APAE video exists yet, use a reversible neutral proposal treatment.

## Partners

Show the partner section near the end of Home.

Do not invent actual partner names/logos.

Use neutral proposal placeholders or a clearly conceptual treatment.

## Testimonials

Demonstrate the module visually without attributing fictional quotes to real people.

## Donation

Demonstrate one-time/monthly support UX and payment-method readiness without executing real payments.

## Portal Urubici

Include a premium "Inclusão & Desenvolvimento Humano" editorial block as a conceptual integration area.

Do not hardcode unvalidated named authors as institutional truth.

## Urubici Connect

Use a discreet footer-level technology partnership line, proposal wording subject to approval:

"Parceira tecnológica: Urubici Connect"

## Accessibility

Preserve WCAG 2.2 AA-oriented behaviors:
- semantic hierarchy;
- contrast;
- keyboard/focus;
- reduced motion;
- responsive text/media;
- accessible forms.

## Quality gate

The proposal is acceptable only if:
- mobile feels primary;
- desktop feels intentionally designed;
- no fabricated institutional facts appear;
- design feels premium;
- modules align with Foundation;
- provisional design decisions remain reversible.


## Brand identity update

The supplied APAE Urubici logo is now the visual anchor for proposal implementation.

Readdy must evolve the proposal toward a digital brand system derived from the logo:
- sunflower/golden yellow;
- leaf green and deeper accessible green;
- warm gray;
- ivory/off-white;
- charcoal.

Prepare for a revitalized digital logo treatment and favicon while preserving the flower, hands, stem/leaves and institutional essence.

Fraunces + Manrope is the current W01P proposal typography standard.

Do not treat the current terracotta/moss palette as protected. It should be migrated toward the logo-derived palette while preserving the existing premium composition.

## Multilingual requirement

The public Site must be multilingual by default with:
- pt-BR (default);
- English;
- Spanish;
- German.

Provide an accessible language selector in desktop header and mobile navigation.

The proposal may keep Portuguese as the only fully authored language initially, but the architecture must support all four locales without duplicating page logic.
