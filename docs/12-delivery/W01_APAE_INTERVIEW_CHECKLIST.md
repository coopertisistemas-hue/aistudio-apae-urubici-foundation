# W01 — APAE Urubici Institutional Interview Checklist

Use this document during the meeting with the APAE Urubici president/authorized representative.

Keep the conversation simple. Do not expose unnecessary technical detail.

---

## 1. Institutional identity

- [ ] Confirm official institution name for public use.
- [ ] Confirm official logo files available.
- [ ] Confirm whether a brand manual exists.
- [ ] Confirm official colors, if formally defined.
- [ ] Confirm preferred institutional descriptor/tagline, if any.
- [ ] Confirm whether the current public SiteArgus content is considered institutionally valid.

### Evidence to collect
- vector logo (SVG/PDF/AI/EPS if available);
- transparent PNG;
- brand guide/manual;
- official stationery or social templates if no guide exists.

---

## 2. Leadership and team

- [ ] Confirm current president.
- [ ] Confirm current board/diretoria.
- [ ] Confirm which leadership roles may be published.
- [ ] Confirm operational team/professional areas that may appear publicly.
- [ ] Confirm whether individual staff photos are authorized.

### Required fields
- full name;
- role;
- optional short bio;
- optional photo;
- publication authorization.

---

## 3. Current services / areas of work

Ask: **What does APAE Urubici actually provide today?**

Validate each active area.

Possible categories for discussion only:
- Education
- Health
- Social assistance
- Rights advocacy
- Family support
- Culture
- Sport
- Employment / autonomy
- Community activities

Do not mark a category active merely because another APAE offers it.

For each approved area:
- [ ] official name;
- [ ] short description;
- [ ] target audience;
- [ ] contact/referral path if applicable;
- [ ] current/active status.

---

## 4. Impact indicators

Ask which numbers APAE is comfortable publishing.

Examples:
- people served;
- families supported;
- professionals;
- projects;
- years of operation;
- annual activities;
- other institutionally meaningful indicators.

For every number:
- [ ] exact value;
- [ ] reference period;
- [ ] source;
- [ ] update frequency;
- [ ] approved for public display.

Never use approximate or remembered figures without confirmation.

---

## 5. Projects and campaigns

- [ ] List active projects.
- [ ] List recurring projects.
- [ ] List upcoming campaigns.
- [ ] Identify closed/historical projects worth preserving.

For each:
- name;
- objective;
- period;
- responsible area;
- media;
- partners;
- public CTA;
- donation eligibility;
- approved impact/result data.

---

## 6. Partners

- [ ] Current official partners.
- [ ] Logo authorization.
- [ ] Website/social link authorization.
- [ ] Supported project(s).
- [ ] Partnership period, if relevant.
- [ ] Whether partner can be featured on Home.
- [ ] Whether APAE wants a featured-partner editorial model.
- [ ] Recognition wording approved.

Clarify whether there are categories such as:
- institutional;
- recurring;
- project sponsor;
- supporter;
- public-sector partner.

Do not create commercial tiers unless APAE explicitly wants them.

---

## 7. Donations and fundraising

Confirm current real-world process.

- [ ] Does APAE currently receive PIX donations?
- [ ] Official PIX key/account owner.
- [ ] Does APAE have PayPal?
- [ ] Does APAE have Stripe?
- [ ] Does APAE want recurring monthly contributions?
- [ ] Who is authorized to view donation data?
- [ ] Who reconciles donations financially?
- [ ] Preferred donor thank-you sender/signature.
- [ ] Is a receipt/declaration workflow needed?
- [ ] Can donations be linked to specific campaigns?

Do not copy credentials into Foundation documentation.

Record only account/provider ownership and implementation status.

---

## 8. Testimonials

Confirm the desired model.

Proposed:
1. Admin-created testimonial;
2. public form;
3. private invitation link via WhatsApp/email.

Validate:
- [ ] APAE approves public testimonial collection.
- [ ] APAE approves moderation before publication.
- [ ] APAE approves separate consent for text/name/photo/social reuse.
- [ ] Who may approve testimonials?
- [ ] Can family members submit?
- [ ] Can partners/volunteers/professionals submit?
- [ ] Any categories that must not be publicly solicited?

---

## 9. Photos and videos

- [ ] Existing institutional photo archive.
- [ ] Existing video archive.
- [ ] Who owns the media?
- [ ] Which assets are cleared for website use?
- [ ] Which are cleared for social reuse?
- [ ] Which feature minors?
- [ ] Which feature beneficiaries/users?
- [ ] Existing consent/image-release records.
- [ ] Media that must never be used.

### Hero video
Ask whether APAE has:
- landscape video;
- vertical/mobile video;
- institutional footage;
- event footage;
- permission to produce/edit a new institutional hero video.

---

## 10. Transparency

Ask what APAE wants publicly available.

- [ ] Current statute.
- [ ] Annual reports.
- [ ] Financial statements.
- [ ] Agreements/convênios.
- [ ] Certificates.
- [ ] Public accountability documents.
- [ ] Institutional policies.
- [ ] Board/diretoria records appropriate for publication.

For every document:
- year/period;
- category;
- approved for publication;
- file owner/source.

---

## 11. Contact and service information

Confirm:
- [ ] address;
- [ ] phone;
- [ ] WhatsApp;
- [ ] institutional email;
- [ ] public service hours;
- [ ] contact responsible;
- [ ] Google profile / Maps information;
- [ ] official social channels.

---

## 12. Social media and communications

- [ ] Official Instagram.
- [ ] Official Facebook.
- [ ] Other official channels.
- [ ] Who has admin access?
- [ ] Who approves publication?
- [ ] Does APAE want newsletter/email communication?
- [ ] Does APAE want WhatsApp relationship communication?
- [ ] Is there an existing mailing/contact list?
- [ ] Does that list have known consent provenance?

Never import an old list blindly.

---

## 13. Portal Urubici integration

Present the concept in non-technical terms.

### Proposed editorial bridge
**Inclusão & Desenvolvimento Humano**

- [ ] APAE approves displaying selected educational/editorial articles from Portal Urubici.
- [ ] APAE approves the editorial relationship.
- [ ] APAE approves attribution/linking back to Portal.
- [ ] Confirm whether Aline Liz content may be surfaced when contextually appropriate.
- [ ] Confirm whether selected APAE events/campaigns/news may appear on Portal Urubici.

No blanket automatic syndication without governance.

---

## 14. Urubici Connect credential

Proposed discreet attribution:

**Parceira tecnológica: Urubici Connect**

Validate:
- [ ] APAE approves the wording or equivalent.
- [ ] Footer placement is acceptable.
- [ ] APAE authorizes future case-study use.
- [ ] Define any restrictions on case-study publication.

The APAE identity remains primary.

---

## 15. Privacy / LGPD / analytics

Ask operational questions, not legal conclusions.

- [ ] Does APAE already have a privacy policy?
- [ ] Is there a DPO/privacy contact or responsible person?
- [ ] Who should handle privacy requests?
- [ ] Does APAE want audience analytics?
- [ ] Does APAE approve a consent mechanism if required by the selected tools?
- [ ] Any existing data-retention rules?
- [ ] Any existing image-consent procedure?

Final legal/privacy text remains subject to appropriate review.

---

## 16. Priorities

Ask the president to rank:

- [ ] Institutional credibility
- [ ] Donations
- [ ] Communication/news
- [ ] Partners
- [ ] Projects/campaigns
- [ ] Volunteer acquisition
- [ ] Transparency
- [ ] Social-media reach
- [ ] Family/community information
- [ ] Other

Ask:

**If this project is a success in 12 months, what has changed for APAE Urubici?**

Record the answer verbatim as stakeholder input.

---

## Meeting closure

Before ending:
- summarize decisions;
- list documents/files APAE will send;
- list open questions;
- agree on one contact person;
- agree that nothing unvalidated will be published.
