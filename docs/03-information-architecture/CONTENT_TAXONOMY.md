# Content Taxonomy

## Editorial types
- News
- Article
- Event
- Project
- Campaign
- Story
- Testimonial
- Transparency document

## Suggested editorial topics
- Inclusion
- Human development
- Accessibility
- Education
- Health
- Social assistance
- Family
- Rights
- Autonomy
- Culture
- Sport
- Community
- Employment and opportunity
- Institutional
- Partnerships

## Source/origin
Content should carry origin when relevant:
- APAE Urubici
- Portal Urubici
- institutional partner
- external reference

## Distribution flags
Where enabled:
- public site
- Portal candidate
- Instagram candidate
- Facebook candidate
- newsletter candidate

Distribution flags must not bypass editorial approval.
