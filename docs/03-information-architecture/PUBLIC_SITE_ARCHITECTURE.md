## Navigation vs sitemap
Primary navigation is an intentional subset of the full route set in `SITE_MAP.md`. Routes not surfaced in primary navigation (e.g. `/parceiros`, `/campanhas`, `/historias`, `/depoimentos`, `/doar`, `/voluntariado`, `/seja-parceiro`) are reached via Home sections, contextual CTAs, or the footer. "Notícias & Artigos" is a grouped navigation label covering the distinct `/noticias` and `/artigos` routes.

## Portal integration concept

The Site may consume selected editorial content from Portal Urubici, especially the Inclusion & Human Development axis.

APAE-originated content may also be exposed selectively to Portal Urubici through governed integration.
