# Homepage Specification

## 1. Hero
Premium full-bleed video or image experience.
Admin-managed desktop/mobile video, posters, copy, CTAs and overlay.
Must preserve legibility and performance.

## 2. Institutional introduction
Short, confident summary of APAE Urubici with link to full institutional page.

## 3. Impact
Validated indicators only. Numbers must be Admin-managed and may be hidden until verified.

## 4. Areas of work
Visual cards for approved service/mission areas. Each leads to its own detail page.

## 5. Projects and campaigns
Current priority initiatives with image, concise purpose, status and CTA.

## 6. Stories / testimonials
Approved social proof. No unmoderated content.

## 7. APAE news
Latest institutional publications.

## 8. Inclusion & Human Development
Curated editorial bridge to Portal Urubici for this axis. Any specific Portal editorial identity referenced for this axis (e.g. "Aline Liz") must be validated for identity, ownership and approval-to-surface before implementation (see Deferred Decisions Register: DD-10). Until validated, surface Portal content generically by the Inclusion & Human Development tag/approval only.

## 9. How to help
Donation, volunteering, partnership and advocacy/sharing paths.

## 10. Donation emphasis
Clear one-time/monthly support CTA and payment options when enabled.

## 11. Partners
Premium recognition section immediately before the closing page area/near footer. Calm, respectful and non-advertorial.

## 12. Final CTA
Human institutional message and next action.

## 13. Footer
Institutional contacts, social links, transparency, policies, accessibility and discreet Urubici Connect technology-partner credential.
